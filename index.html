Cut points

Parts are virtual segments of one file — no re-encoding. The app seeks to start_ms and stops at end_ms. Drag the green (start) and red (end) markers on the timeline, or type times as mm:ss.

Auto-suggest: Part 1 = 0:00–3:00, then 60-second segments (Part 2: 3:00–4:00, Part 3: 4:00–5:00, …), clipped to the video length. Preview plays 10s before the start to 10s after the end.
